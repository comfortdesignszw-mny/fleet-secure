# Fleet Secure — Staff User Guide

Welcome to **Fleet Secure**, our digital yard record book. This guide explains how every staff member uses the app on their phone or computer.

---

## 1. Signing In & Changing Your PIN
1. Open the Fleet Secure app (you can tap **Install App** to put an icon on your phone’s home screen).
2. Enter your **Username** and **PIN / Password**.
3. If this is your first login—or if an Admin reset your PIN—you will be asked to choose a **new private PIN** (at least 4 characters). Never share your PIN with anyone else.

---

## 2. Yard Operations (Drivers, Clerks & Supervisors)

On the **Home** screen you have 4 large buttons:

### A. Hire Out a Vehicle (Customer Rental)
1. Tap **Hire Out a Vehicle**.
2. Select the **Driver** and type the **Client Name**.
3. Choose the **Vehicle** (grounded or already-out cars do not appear).
4. Type the exact **Mileage when leaving** shown on the dashboard.
   - *Note:* If the number differs from the car’s last saved mileage, the app warns you and notifies your Supervisor.
5. Tap the picture on the **Fuel gauge** that matches the needle (`Full`, `3/4+`, `3/4`, `1/2`, etc.).
6. Enter who did the opening checklist, tap **Review & Save**, check every line carefully, and tap **Confirm & Save Permanently**.

### B. Return a Hired Vehicle
1. Tap **Return a Hired Vehicle** (or tap **Record Return** next to the car under **Ongoing Trips**).
2. Enter the exact **Mileage when returning**.
3. Tap the **Fuel when returning** gauge level.
4. Check the automatic **Total Mileage** and **Estimated Fuel** boxes.
5. Enter who did the closing checklist, review, and tap **Confirm & Save Return**.

### C. Send on Errand & Errand Returned
- Same simple steps as Hire Out / Return, except instead of Client Name you enter **Destination** and **Reason for Errand** (e.g. *Parts pickup*, *ZIMRA*).

## 3. Common Questions

### "I made a mistake after pressing Save. What do I do?"
Saved trip entries are **permanently locked** and cannot be edited or deleted.  
Tell your **Supervisor (Loss Control)** right away. They can open the trip and add an official **Correction Entry** with the right number and a written reason. Both the original entry and the correction stay visible.

### "There is no internet signal in the yard!"
Fill in and confirm the trip as normal. It will save to your phone under **Waiting to send** on the Home screen and upload automatically as soon as internet comes back.

### "Why is a trip highlighted in red?"
Fleet Secure raises automatic alerts when it spots:
- **UNRECORDED_GAP:** Odometer changed while the car was supposedly parked.
- **FUEL_MISMATCH:** Gauge fuel drop does not match the kilometres driven.
- **OVERDUE:** A client hire stayed out longer than the allowed hours.
- **LICENCE_EXPIRING:** The driver’s licence has expired or expires within 30 days.

---

## 4. Supervisor & Admin Responsibilities
- **Vehicles & Drivers:** Keep vehicle conditions (`Good`, `Bad Condition`, `Accident`, `Grounded`) and driver licence expiry dates up to date.
- **Alerts Inbox:** Check unread red alerts and tap any alert to jump straight to the trip.
- **Reports:** Open **Reports** to download **CSV (Excel)** or **PDF** sheets stamped with company details.
- **Audit Log:** Open **Audit Log** and tap **Verify Audit Trail** regularly to confirm zero tampering.
- **Settings (Admin only):** Update company details, tank sizes / km-per-litre fuel rates, staff accounts, and Email/WhatsApp alert recipients.
